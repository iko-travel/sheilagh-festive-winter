I've looked at all the options for your nights in Vienna. Unfortunately, most of the nicer places within walking distance of Hotel Das Tigra are either fully booked or very expensive for those dates.

Here are the four best options I found. Each one keeps you close to Das Tigra and the old town. Have a look and let me know which feels right for you.

Option 1

Stay at Das Tigra, with one night next door

We book Das Tigra for Friday and Sunday night and keep watching for a cancellation on the Saturday. If no room opens up, you spend Saturday night at Dan in 'n Out, just a 3-minute walk away. It isn't as nice as Das Tigra, but it's the closest comfortable option.
